Expert Mode

MadMapper includes a dedicated Expert Mode designed for advanced users, permanent installations, and complex show control networks. When enabled in the MadMapper Preferences, this mode unhides a variety of highly specific system controls, rendering optimizations, and advanced routing capabilities that are typically hidden to keep the standard interface streamlined.

To enable these features, navigate to your Preferences menu and check the box for Expert Mode.

 

Application & System Controls

Expert Mode exposes powerful system-level commands, which is particularly useful for controlling unattended or permanent architectural installations via OSC, MIDI, or DMX.

  • System Commands: You gain access to trigger the following core commands remotely:

    • /application/exit (Quits MadMapper)

    • /application/shutdown_computer (Turns off the machine)

    • /application/restart_computer (Reboots the machine)

Advanced Rendering & Outputs

Expert mode unlocks deeper control over how MadMapper processes and publishes pixel data, allowing you to optimize your graphics pipeline.

  • Materials (Render to Texture): The specific Pixel Format dropdown is unhidden, allowing you to manually define the exact bit-depth and color channels for your rendered materials.

  • Video Loopbacks: The Pixel Format settings for internal loopbacks are exposed. (Note: When Expert Mode is disabled, loopbacks default automatically to standard RGBA 8 bpc).

  • Dynamic Output Controls: It enables mappable controls for your video outputs, allowing you to dynamically toggle the publishing states on the fly. This exposes:

    • show_desktop_window

    • publish_to_syphon_spout

    • publish_to_ndi

Geometry & Layer Optimizations

For intricate mapping and laser routing, Expert Mode provides additional parameters for vector-based surfaces.

  • Lines & Laser Lines: * Geometry Precision Slider: Becomes available to manually fine-tune the mathematical resolution and vertex count of your line paths.

    • Dynamic Bounding Rect: This option is unhidden. It is an advanced optimization setting that forces the software to dynamically recalculate the exact bounding box of animated or changing line geometry frame-by-frame, which can be critical for highly specific tracking or masking scenarios.

Advanced Timeline Features

Expert Mode fundamentally expands how Timelines can be structured and synchronized, removing the standard limitations of the MAD Conductor.

  • Nested Timeline Players: You are no longer restricted to placing Timeline Player tracks only on the master Conductor. Expert mode allows you to add Timeline Player tracks directly inside other individual timelines, enabling complex, nested sequencing.

  • Per-Timeline External Sync: You can assign External Sync (such as SMPTE/LTC or MIDI Timecode) directly to individual timelines, allowing different parts of your show to sync to different external sources simultaneously without relying solely on the master Conductor.

Note: Enabling Expert Mode also unlocks a variety of advanced calibration and rendering features specific to the Laser Module. These specific parameters are detailed in the advanced sections of the MadLaser documentation.
