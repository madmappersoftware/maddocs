Extensions & Companions

To expand the core capabilities of the software, the MadMapper ecosystem includes specialized extensions and companion hardware designed for specific professional workflows:

MadLaser

 

MadLaser is an extension of MadMapper dedicated to laser mapping. Compatible with multiple laser hardware devices, MadLaser transforms any shape, text, or generative content into laser trajectories in real time. Create fluid, sharp, and expressive animations directly synchronized with video, audio, or lighting.

MadLaser Key Features

  • Unlimited laser outputs: As many as your computer can handle
  • Multi Hardware supported: Ether Dream, ShowNET, Helios, FB3, FB4 (with Pangolin and Windows), AVB/Dante, LaserCube, IDN
  • Laser Bezier lines: Ultra-precise vector graphics
  • 3D object rendering from .OBJ format

  • Real-Time Video Vectorization: Hardware-accelerated image analysis and processing.

  • Vector Materials: Real-time, shader-based generative laser content.

  • High-Speed Scanning: Supports up to 100 kpps scanners for high-definition graphics.

  • Advanced Routing: Laser Soft Edge on quads, and Laser Dispatch to spread complex content across multiple projectors.

  • Visualization & Safety: Robust pre-visualization capabilities and critical safety zoning.

  • ILDA Support: Import and export standard ILDA files.

MadLaser is an extension of MadMapper and cannot operate as a standalone application.

A MadMapper license is required.

MadProjectorControl

 

The MadProjectorControl extension uses the PJLink protocol to remotely control any number of compatible video projectors. Fully cueable, this extension allows you to schedule and automate power and shutter activity directly from your mapping station.

 

Main features:

  • Compatible with any PJLink-compliant device.

  • Control and monitor an unlimited number of projectors.

  • Cueable, schedulable, and automatable.

  • Remote Power On/Off and Shutter control.

  • Displays live device information and lamp status.

Like MadLaser, MadProjectorControl is an extension and requires a MadMapper license.

MadAI

 

Transform ideas into dynamic visuals without writing a single line of code. Whether you need material (shader) generation, 2D effects, or laser animations, simply type a prompt and MadAI will take care of the rest. You can also use AI to remix existing content from your library to find new inspiration.

MadAI requires credits to make requests!

Tip: Looking for inspiration before generating your own? Explore the MadMaterials online public library directly within the software to access an extensive collection of shaders and effects created and shared by the worldwide MadMapper community.

MiniMad

 

MiniMad is a hardware plug-and-play device designed to play back your MadMapper setups with automatic network synchronization for multi-projection, or DMX/Art-Net output to control lighting and LED equipment.

 

MiniMad makes your installation easy and affordable: it hooks up to a projector and plays content on surfaces exported from MadMapper. Alternatively, you can achieve an equivalent outcome through light mapping. Small by size but feature-rich, the MiniMad allows you to export quad, triangle, circle, or mask surfaces as well as their content from a selected projector in MadMapper. You can also switch the MiniMad to lighting mode and output DMX/Art-Net channel data to your light fixtures.

 

Discreet and Autonomous

The MiniMad can run on its own for a very long time—units in the lab have run continuously for more than a year. In case of a power failure, the MiniMad automatically restarts without any user intervention. All the mapping modifications you do are automatically saved on the SD card.

 

Team Power

Multiple MiniMads can be synchronized with each other over Ethernet to make a cluster of synchronized video players. Zero configuration is needed; MadMapper will automatically split movies and optimize files for playback.

 

MiniMad Main Features

  • Automatic synchronization of video media playback when multiple MiniMads are connected to the same Ethernet network.

  • In-depth quality export with precise video controls.

  • Up to 64 universes for Art-Net/DMX.

  • Autonomous playback of content recorded from MadMapper.

  • Multi-channel audio when using Light mode.

  • Native video playback up to 1080p30 in optimal conditions.

  • Physical controls with friendly buttons to tweak your mapping directly on location.

  • Video or Light: 2 playback modes available.

  • VideoWall easy setup—a one-click, fully automated process.

  • Zero configuration, as simple as that.

  • Playback modes: loop, random, list... configure your MiniMad to play back your videos exactly the way you want.

  • Mesh Warping and Perspective Pinning with adjustable manual controls.

  • Remote control your MiniMads directly from MadMapper.

 

Technical Specifications

  • Raspberry Pi 2
  • 7 buttons control panel
  • 1x HDMI output up to 1080p (1920x1080 at 60Hz)
  • Size: 9 x 6 x 3 cm (approx. 3.5 x 2.3 x 1.2 inches)

  • MiniMad is setup for either video mapping or light mapping; it cannot do both at the same time. Use different units to do so.

  • Media playback (video) is automatically synced when MiniMad units are connected to the same Ethernet network. This can induce a one-frame delay between the different units. Therefore the device is not suitable for blending/soft-edge.

  • For MiniMads that are running continuously for months, i.e., for an installation, we recommend using industrial-grade Micro SD cards as well as having backup SD cards with your project already exported.
