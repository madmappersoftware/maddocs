DMX Fixtures

Basics

DMX fixtures allow you to create and manage your fixtures for your light mapping projets. By adding fixtures you will need somehow to replicate your physical lighting installation in MadMapper.

Since MadMapper 6, DMX fixtures are sampling the output!

Location

Access the DMX fixtures by clicking on DMX (manage DMX fixtures) at the top left of the interface:

 

New DMX Fixture

Simply click on the DMX fixture of your choice to add one:

 

Created fixtures retain the same profile and follow the addressing. More info below as well as in the MadLight Documentation.

DMX Fixtures List

Added DMX fixtures (and groups) will be listed on the left in the DMX fixtures List:

 

Simply double-click on a DMX fixture name to rename it.

 

Other options are :

  • Display / Un-display a DMX fixture.
  • Lock / Unlock a DMX fixture.

Locking a DMX fixture is useful when you don’t want to change its settings anymore, it will avoid accidents.

 

The highlighted DMX fixture or group is also the one that is selected.

Note that DMX Fixtures can be moved around in the fixtures list, but a fixture will overwrite the one below it because there are no opacity settings for fixtures.

You can’t define two fixtures on top of each other and play with the opacity, it will «overwrite» it instead.

Create Groups

You can create a group with as many DMX fixtures as you want by clicking on the Groups icon at top right of the DMX fixture list:

 

This is very useful for grouping your fixtures by universes.

Note that you can also drag your surfaces directly into a group, no matter what type they are.

Or select a group of surfaces, then click on the group icon holding the Shift key. A group will be created with the selected surfaces, in the same location as the selected surfaces.

As with Video Surfaces, DMX fixtures behave as layers as well.

DMX Fixture Inspector

The DMX Fixture Inspector, below the DMX fixture list, displays the important parameters of the selected DMX fixture (highlighted in the fixtures list).

The settings vary depending on the type of DMX fixture.

The percentage of a setting is also multiplied by the group setting.

Fixture Definition

Choose one from the Fixture library and eventually click on Edit to open the Fixture Editor panel.

 

Once our profile is selected, the information of our fixture is updated in the fixture list.

These default profiles are particularly useful, especially the native garageCube ones.
However, note that you can define your own custom fixtures in the Fixture Editor.
