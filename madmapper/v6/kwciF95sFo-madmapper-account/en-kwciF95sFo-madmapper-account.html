MadMapper User Account

MadMapper requires a user account to be used. You will need it to activate the software on a computer.

 

Your license(s) will be associated with this account, allowing you to manage your authorizations, updates, and more.

Create a user account

 

The user account portal can only be accessed from the MadMapper website, at the top right menu by clicking on the icon on the left.

 

 

 

You can sign in using your existing MadMapper account or create a new one by selecting the option at the bottom of the screen.

Your MadMapper account is different from your garageCube forum account.

 

Choose your username carefully! It cannot be changed later, unlike your email address or password.

If you do not receive the confirmation email to validate your account, check first your spam folder.

Licenses

Now we’re getting down to business!

This page displays all your licenses: perpetual, temporary (including expired ones), as well as those for extensions such as MadLaser, and MadAI credits.

 

For each of your licenses, you have the option to upgrade it whenever an upgrade is available.

 

Each license displays important information such as the Order Number (or Redeem Key ID), the license serial number, and the computers authorized with that license:

 

 

If you no longer have access to a computer you need to de-authorize, you can revoke its authorization remotely using a Reset operation.

As the Reset operation is an emergency procedure, its use is limited: if you use a Reset token for one of your licenses, you'll have to wait a year to get your Reset token back for that license.

Please note that you can recover a Reset token by launching MadMapper on the computer for which you used the Reset operation.

Import

 

If you own a MadMapper 1 through 4 license, simply enter your serial number using the provided field and click IMPORT.

Your license will be automatically added to your account and will be ready for immediate use.

Redeem keys function as activation codes. When you import an RK, the system automatically generates the corresponding license and assigns it to your account.

Please note that a license can only be linked to one account at a time.

If you receive an error message such as “License already imported”, it means the license is already associated with another account.

In that case, please contact our support team for assistance.

If you acquire MadMapper now, your license will be automatically linked to your account. No further action is required.

Downloads

From the Downloads tab, you can access the MadMapper installers as well as additional resources related to the software.

 

Since MadMapper 5, three versions of the software are available: Windows, macOS Intel, and macOS Apple Silicon.

If you’re unsure which one to download, simply verify your computer’s system information to determine the correct version.
