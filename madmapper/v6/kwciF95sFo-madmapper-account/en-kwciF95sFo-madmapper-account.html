MadMapper User Account

MadMapper requires each user to create a personal account.

Your license(s) will be linked directly to this account, allowing you to manage your authorizations, updates, and more.

This ensures that your software access stays organized and easily recoverable across any devices.

Create a user account

 

The user account can only be accessed from the MadMapper website, at the top right menu by clicking on the icon on the left.

 

You can then sign in using your existing MadMapper account, or create a new one by selecting the option at the bottom of the screen.

Your MadMapper account is different from your garageCube forum account

Choose your username carefully, it cannot be changed later, unlike your email address or password.

 

Once your account has been validated after clicking on the link in the email you received, you're good to go!

Import

 

If you own a MadMapper 1 through 4 license, simply enter your serial number using the provided field and click IMPORT.

Your license will be automatically added to your account and will be ready for immediate use.

Redeem keys function as activation codes. When you import an RK, the system automatically generates the corresponding license and assigns it to your account.

Please note that a license can only be linked to one account at a time.

If you receive an error message such as “License already imported”, it means the license is already associated with another account.

In that case, please contact our support team for assistance.

If you acquire MadMapper now, your license will be automatically linked to your account. No further action is required.
