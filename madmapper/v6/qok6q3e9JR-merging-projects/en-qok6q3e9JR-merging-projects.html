Merging Projects

The Merge Project... feature allows you to seamlessly import the contents of one MadMapper project directly into another.

This feature is incredibly useful for collaborative workflows, combining different elements of a show (like bringing a lighting designer's DMX fixture patch into a video operator's mapping file), or pulling a standardized template of surfaces into a brand-new venue setup without starting from scratch.

How to Merge Projects

To combine two projects, follow these steps:

  1. Open your Primary Project: Launch MadMapper and load the master .madproject workspace that you want to serve as the foundation of your show.

  2. Select Merge: Navigate to the top menu bar and select File > Merge Project...

  3. Choose the Source File: A file browser window will appear. Navigate to the secondary .mad file (or .madproject folder) that you wish to import and select it.

  4. Importing: MadMapper will process the selected file and append its contents directly into your current, active workspace.

What Gets Merged?

When you merge a project, MadMapper acts cumulatively, bringing in the elements of the secondary project without deleting or overwriting the existing data in your primary project. The merged components include:

  • Surfaces and Geometry: All video, light, and laser surfaces (Quads, Triangles, Lines, 3D Objects), retaining their input/output geometry, masks, and mesh warping.

  • Fixtures: All patched DMX fixtures, universes, and settings.

  • Media: Video files, images, generators, and materials loaded into the Media Bin.

  • Scenes and Cues: All programmed cues, scene states, and timeline data from the imported project.

  • Controls: Existing MIDI, OSC, and DMX mapping configurations linked to the imported setup.

Tip: After a successful merge, imported surfaces and fixtures will typically appear in their respective lists in the Tool Panels, and new media will be appended to your Media Bin. Organizing the imported items into dedicated Groups immediately after merging can help keep your interface clean.

Workspace Organization & Best Practices

Because MadMapper 6 utilizes a self-contained .madproject folder structure, merging a project from a different location on your hard drive means your primary project will now be temporarily referencing media files that live outside of its own workspace folder.

To maintain perfect project portability and organization, you should always consolidate your assets after a merge:

  1. Once your projects are merged, go to File > Collect External Resources...

  2. MadMapper will automatically locate all the newly imported media assets (videos, images, 3D files) and copy them directly into your primary .madproject Resources folder.

This guarantees that if you move your newly merged show file to a show computer or external drive, absolutely no media links will be broken.
