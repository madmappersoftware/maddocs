Advanced MadMapper Configuration

The Advanced section explores powerful extensions, specialized tools, and system optimizations designed to push MadMapper beyond standard workflows. Here you will learn how to leverage generative AI for content creation, automate projector management using MadProjectorControl, and interface with physical microcontrollers via Arduino and Firmata. This section also covers critical best practices for maximizing render performance, executing spatial auto-calibration, and ensuring project security with robust backup and recovery strategies, equipping you for complex, large-scale installations.
