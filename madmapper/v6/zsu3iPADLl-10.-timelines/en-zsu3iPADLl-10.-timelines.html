Timelines

The Timelines section introduces the powerful sequencing and playback tools at the heart of your MadMapper projects. Here you will learn how to sequence your show using timeline-based playback, automate parameters with keyframe interpolation, and perfectly synchronize video and lighting to audio tracks. This section covers everything from managing Scenes and Animation Clips to utilizing the Conductor for a bird's-eye view of your show, and setting up Time Markers for advanced, interactive follow actions.
