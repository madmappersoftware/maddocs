Updating from MadMapper 5

Updating your workflow from MadMapper 5 to MadMapper 6 requires adjusting to the new timeline-based system and the updated project workspace structure.

When opening a MadMapper 5 project in MadMapper 6, the software is designed to automatically and safely translate your existing setup into the new engine without overwriting your original files.

Migration Guide

1. Opening and Converting a Legacy Project Updating a project from MadMapper 5 to MadMapper 6 will automatically put the new project into a self-contained .madproject folder that you can assign when converting.

 

Workspace Path: When you first open the .mad file, you will be prompted to choose a directory for this new workspace. You can Browse for a specific location or Reset to default (which places it in your Documents / MadMapper folder).

 

2. Safe Conversion & Saving Once a path is selected, MadMapper will display a confirmation warning detailing how the file is being handled:

  • The old MadMapper 5 project remains completely intact and can still be loaded using MadMapper 5.

  • The new project is loaded as an "Untitled" project until you save it.

  • Action Required: Immediately go to File > Save Project As... to give your newly converted show file a name and secure it within its new workspace.

3. Consolidate Your Assets Because your legacy project was just imported, it is likely still referencing media scattered across your hard drive rather than utilizing the new folder structure.

  • Action Required: Navigate to File > Collect External Resources... to copy all your existing V5 media directly into the new, localized .madproject folder.

4. Review Cues and Logic The traditional snapshot-style Cues from version 5 have been fundamentally replaced by dynamic Timelines and Clips in version 6.

  • Action Required: Your previous Cues will be automatically adapted to fit the new Timeline structure. You must manually review your show control logic—especially MIDI or OSC triggers that previously fired specific Cues—to ensure they are correctly mapped to the new Timeline or MAD Clips playback.

5. Verify Preferences and Routing The preferences menu has been reorganized. Settings are now strictly divided between Application Settings (global hardware routing, audio devices, etc.) and Project Settings (show-specific safety limits, OSC/MIDI outputs).

  • Action Required: Verify your hardware and network routing in the Preferences menu (Cmd + , or Ctrl + ,) before arming your outputs or lasers.
