What You Need to Run MadMapper

MadMapper is designed to be lightweight, flexible, and compatible with a wide range of hardware configurations.

Whether you’re working on a small projection mapping project or a large-scale installation, here’s what you need to know about supported systems and recommended setups.

 

At its core, all you need to get started with MadMapper is a computer running macOS or Windows

Also meaning even with the Demo version you can explore the interface, design your mappings, and experiment with ideas entirely on-screen.

 

The minimum hardware requirements to run MadMapper is a computer running macOS or Windows

At least 300 MB of disk space and a minimum of 2 GB of RAM and a GPU compatible with OpenGL 3.3.

 

We recommend a CPU with at least 2–4 cores and a minimum of 8 GB of RAM. An SSD and a dedicated GPU are also highly recommended for optimal performance.

 

MadMapper 6 runs seamlessly on both macOS and Windows, offering the same powerful performances and features across platforms.

macOS: version 11 or higher

Windows: version 10 or higher

 

There is no “better” platform, both systems are fully supported and deliver excellent performance.

 

Your choice will mostly come down to personal preference, budget, and the kind of projects you plan to create. Some artists prefer macOS for its streamlined workflow and system stability, while others choose Windows for its flexibility and broader hardware options.

 

Ultimately, both platforms let you get the most out of MadMapper’s creative potential. 

macOS Recommendations

MadMapper performs well even on modest Apple hardware, making it an excellent choice for portability and reliability.

  • Entry-Level: Standard MacBooks (such as the MacBook Air or older MacBook Pro models) are perfectly fine for common projection mapping tasks. Even a second-hand MacBook can be suitable for light to medium use.

  • Heavy Workloads: If you plan to work extensively with heavy shaders, multiple high-resolution video inputs, or large 3D content, you will benefit significantly from a more powerful GPU.

  • Apple Silicon: MadMapper has been written as a native M-software for Apple Silicon (M-series) computers since version 5, ensuring maximum efficiency on modern Macs.

MadMapper has been a native M-software for Apple Silicon (M-series) computers since version 5.

Windows Recommendations

MadMapper runs equally well on Windows systems, provided they are properly configured. Even compact PCs such as Intel NUCs or mini PCs can handle projection mapping surprisingly well, making them ideal for small or portable permanent installations.

  • Processor (CPU): Choose a recent, high-performance model (such as an Intel i5/i7/i9 or AMD Ryzen series). Avoid entry-level CPUs, which can easily bottleneck your performance.

  • Storage: An SSD drive is strongly recommended to ensure fast project loading and smooth video playback.

  • Memory (RAM): At least 8 GB is required, though 16 GB or more is highly recommended for smooth performance when juggling multiple media files.

  • Graphics Card (GPU): A dedicated GPU is essential for heavy video, LED, and laser workloads. Both NVIDIA and AMD cards are fully supported.

    • NVIDIA Driver Recommendation: It is strongly advised to install the NVIDIA Studio Driver rather than the standard "Game Ready" driver. The Studio driver prioritizes absolute stability for creative applications, whereas the Game Ready driver contains game-specific optimization tricks that are unnecessary for MadMapper and can occasionally introduce instability in live environments.

    • Display Stability Note: The most common hardware-related crashes on Windows occur when physically disconnecting or reconnecting displays while the GPU is actively under load. To ensure absolute stability, always physically connect and configure your display outputs before launching MadMapper.
