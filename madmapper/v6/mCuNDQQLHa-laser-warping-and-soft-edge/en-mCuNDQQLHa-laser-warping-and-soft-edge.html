Laser Warping & Soft Edge

Mesh Warping

(Applies to Laser Quads, Lines, and Text)
An essential technique that applies a grid mesh to warp your laser output. Activate Bezier Mesh Warping to use curves to fit your laser perfectly to complex physical surfaces.

Soft-Edge

(Applies to Laser Quads)
Extremely useful when overlapping multiple laser projectors to cover a massive stage. This allows you to blend and fade the intersecting laser zones smoothly.
