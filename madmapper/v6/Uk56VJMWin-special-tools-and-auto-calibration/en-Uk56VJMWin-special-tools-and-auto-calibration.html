Special Tools & Auto-Calibration

When dealing with highly complex stages, scattered LED installations, or intricate architectural mappings, manual calibration can become incredibly time-consuming. MadMapper provides three powerful auto-calibration tools to speed up these advanced workflows.

These features can be accessed from the top menu bar under Tools -> Scanners: Space Scanner, LED Scanner, and Laser Scanner.

Space Scanner

(Formerly the Spatial Scanner)

If you do not have a pre-built 3D model of your stage or building, you can capture it yourself using the Space Scanner.

Using a built-in web camera, an external USB web camera, or a supported Canon DSLR (via the Canon ESDK integration) connected to your computer, MadMapper will project a rapid sequence of structured light patterns (black and white stripes) from your projector onto the physical scene.

The camera photographs these patterns as they shift over the physical contours of your stage. MadMapper then triangulates the data between the camera's lens and the projector's lens to generate a pixel-perfect, warped mapping mesh or "calibration map" automatically. This effectively turns your projector into a high-resolution 3D scanner, allowing you to instantly map complex geometry without placing a single manual warping point.

LED Scanner

When building custom LED installations, placing physical light tubes or pixel tape rarely results in a perfect geometric grid. Trying to manually arrange dozens of virtual DMX fixtures in MadMapper to match their scattered real-world positions is notoriously difficult. The LED Scanner automates this entirely.

  1. Setup: Position a camera (webcam or DSLR) so it has a clear view of your entire physical LED installation.

  2. Configure: Open the LED Scanner, select your camera input, and select the specific DMX fixtures/universes you want to map.

  3. The Scan: When you start the scan, MadMapper will sequentially flash every single LED pixel in your physical installation one by one.

  4. The Result: The camera tracks these flashes in real-time. Once finished, MadMapper automatically arranges all of your virtual DMX fixtures in your MadLight workspace to perfectly mirror their exact physical coordinates in the real world. You can immediately drop a video texture across your workspace and it will map flawlessly across your physical LEDs.

Laser Scanner

Introduced in MadMapper 5.5, the Laser Scanner brings the power of auto-calibration to the MadLaser extension.

Because laser hardware utilizes high-speed mechanical galvanometers (mirrors) rather than pixel grids, aligning laser paths to physical architecture or syncing them perfectly over a video projection traditionally requires intensive, manual bezier warping.

By utilizing the Laser Scanner, a connected camera reads calibration patterns drawn by your physical laser projector. The software calculates the exact physical distortion, angle, and distance of the laser beam, and automatically generates a corrected mapping. This ensures that your vector paths, ILDA files, and laser materials hit your physical targets with absolute precision.
