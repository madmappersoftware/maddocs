Assigning Content to Surfaces

Once your surfaces are created and placed on your stage, you need to map your video or generative content onto them. MadMapper offers several flexible workflows to assign media:

Assigning Media to Existing Surfaces

  • The Media Bin: First, select your target layer in the Surfaces list. Then, simply click the desired media icon in your Media Bin. The surface will instantly update to display that content.

  • The Surface Inspector: Alternatively, with a surface selected, you can navigate to the "Media" section of the Surface Inspector and use the drop-down window to select your source content.

 

The Drag-and-Drop Workflow For the absolute fastest setup, you can drag and drop a media file from your computer directly into the MadMapper stage window. Doing this will automatically create a brand new Quad surface that fits that specific media type, perfectly preserving its original aspect ratio.

 

Input Rectangles and Cropping Assigning the media is only half the process; you also need to define exactly which part of the video is displayed on your surface. When you open the Input View, you will see a rectangle representing your surface hovering over your source media.

By moving, scaling, or adjusting the corners of this Input Rectangle, you can crop the source video. This allows you to pinpoint a very specific region of interest to project onto your real-world object, leaving the rest of the video file hidden.
