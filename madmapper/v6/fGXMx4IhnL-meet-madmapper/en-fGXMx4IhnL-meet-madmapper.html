MadMapper, the Ultimate Mapping Software

 

MadMapper is a leading software for video and light mapping used globally by visual artists, scenographers, and many more. Built for creative freedom and live performance, it allows users to map content onto any surface - flat or complex - in real time.

 

Key features include support for multiple simultaneous inputs/outputs, advanced mapping tools, a built-in library of textures and effects, audio reactivity, real-time video playback, DMX/Art-Net lighting control, sequencing & timeline, export, and laser mapping - enabling users to control laser projectors - with unprecedented accuracy and flexibility.

 

It remains highly accessible to newcomers while offering deep customization for professionals. MadMapper is widely adopted by art schools, institutions, media labs, with training programs around the world, fostering a strong community of learners and creators who shape the future of audiovisual expression.

Video Mapping

 

MadMapper is the reference software for real-time video mapping, combining an intuitive interface with professional technical capabilities.

 

It enables video and generative content to be projected onto simple or complex surfaces, thanks to many advanced mapping tools. Users can precisely map various content onto 2D and 3D volumes with total control.

 

The integrated video engine is ultra-high-performance, capable of handling very high resolutions and playing formats such as HAP, ProRes, standard H.264 and more. It supports multiple simultaneous video outputs, making it ideal for immersive installations and large-scale events.

 

MadMapper also supports live sources via Syphon, Spout, NDI or camera feeds, and offers perfectly fluid playback, whether looped, triggered or timeline-driven.

 

The software also includes a full-featured shader editor, enabling the integration of generative effects in real time, and an audio engine that enables content or effects to be driven from sound (spectrum analysis, beat detection, etc.). Compatibility with standard protocols (MIDI, OSC, DMX/Art-Net, Ableton Link) makes it a particularly connected and powerful creative platform.

Light Mapping

 

MadMapper gives you precise creative control over light thanks to its powerful MadLight feature. The software natively supports DMX, Art-Net, or sACN protocols.

 

Its integrated LED scanner facilitates spatial calibration of installations, and coupled with video, audio or shaders, light mapping in MadMapper paves the way for dynamic, immersive and totally synchronized light scenography.

MadMapper Key Features

  • Ultra fast Video Player: Playback at huge resolutions
  • Multiple projectors: Connect as many projectors as your computer can handle
  • Bezier Masking: Hand-cut, trim and mask your content with advanced Bezier geometry
  • Mesh Warping: Precisely fit your content to real surfaces with professional warping tools
  • Spout / Syphon / NDI: Share content with other graphic applications
  • Live controls: Connect any MIDI/OSC controller to MadMapper and perform in realtime
  • Material Editor: Use the MadMapper shader editor to create your generative texture and share them with the community
  • 3D Calibration: Match your 3D model to the real world
  • Space Scanner: Use your camera to capture the projector’s point of view
  • Built-in generative content: Colors, patterns, grids, text, and generative shaders natively included

All MadMapper features are available with any paid license type - perpetual, temporary, or educational.

MadLaser

 

MadLaser is an extension of MadMapper dedicated to laser mapping. Compatible with multiple Laser hardware devices, MadLaser transforms any shape, text or generative content into laser trajectories in real time, and above all, in true MadMapper fashion.

 

Create fluid, sharp and expressive animations with ease, directly synchronized with video, audio or lighting. Accessible and powerful, the MadLaser extension opens up a new dimension in audiovisual creation.

MadLaser Key Features

  • Unlimited laser outputs: As many as your computer can handle
  • Multi Hardware supported: Ether Dream, ShowNET, Helios, FB3, FB4 (with Pangolin and Windows), AVB/Dante, LaserCube, IDN
  • Laser Bezier lines: Ultra-precise vector graphics
  • 3D object rendering from .OBJ format
  • Realtime video vectorization: Hardware-accelerated image analysis and processing
  • Ilda files import / export
  • Vector Materials: Real-time shader based
  • Up to 100 kpps scanners: High-definition graphics
  • Laser Soft Edge on laser quads
  • Laser Dispatch: Spread complex content over multiple lasers

MadLaser is an extension of MadMapper and cannot operate as a standalone application.

A MadMapper license is required.

MadProjectorControl

 

The MadProjectorControl extension uses the PJLink protocol to remotely control any number of compatible video projectors.

Fully cueable, this extension allows you to schedule and automate power and shutter activity.

 

Main features:

  • Compatible with any PJLink-compliant device
  • Control any number of projectors
  • Cuable, schedulable and automatable
  • Allows power on/off and shutter remotely
  • Displays device information

Like MadLaser, MadProjectorControl is an extension and requires a MadMapper license.

MiniMad

 

MiniMad is a hardware plug-and-play device designed to playback your MadMapper setups with automatic network synchronization for multi-projection or DMX/Art-net output to control lighting and LED equipment.

 

MiniMad makes your installation easy and affordable: it hooks up to a projector and plays content on surfaces exported from MadMapper. Alternatively, you can achieve an equivalent outcome through light mapping.

 

Small by size but feature-rich: With the tiny MiniMad, you export quad, triangle circle or mask surfaces as well as their content from a selected projector in MadMapper. You can also switch the MiniMad to lighting mode and output DMX/Artnet channel data to your light fixtures.

 

Discreet and Autonomous: the MiniMad can run on its own for a very long time: we have one unit in the lab running for more than a year! In case of a power failure, the MiniMad will automatically restart without any user intervention. All the mapping modifications you do are automatically saved on the SD card.

 

Team Power: multiple MiniMads can be synchronized with each other over Ethernet to make a cluster of synchronized video players. Zero configuration needed! MadMapper will automatically split movies and optimize files for playback.

 

MiniMad Main Features

  • Automatic synchronisation of video media playback when multiple MiniMads are connected to the same Ethernet network
  • In-depth quality export with precise video controls
  • Up to 64 universes for ArtNet/DMX
  • Autonomous playback of content recorded from MadMapper
  • Multi-channel audio when using Light mode
  • Native video playback up to 1080p30 in optimal conditions.
  • Physical controls with friendly buttons to tweak your mapping directly on location
  • Video or Light: 2 playback modes available
  • VideoWall easy setup - one click full automated process
  • Zero configuration, as simple as that
  • Playback modes: loop, random, list... configure your MiniMad to playback your videos the way you want
  • Mesh Warping and Perspective Pinning with adjustable manual controls.
  • Remote control your MiniMads directly from MadMapper

 

Technical Specifications

  • Raspberry Pi 2
  • 7 buttons control panel
  • 1x HDMI output up to 1080p (1920x1080 at 60Hz)
  • Size: 9 x 6 x 3 cm (approx. 3.5 x 2.3 x 1.2 inches)
  • MiniMad is setup for either video mapping or light mapping; it cannot do both at the same time. Use different units to do so.
  • Media playback (video) is automatically synced when MiniMad units are connected to the same Ethernet network. This can induce a one-frame delay between the different units. Therefore the device is not suitable for blending/soft-edge.
  • For MiniMads that are running continuously for months, ie. for an installation, we recommend using industrial-grade Micro SD cards as well as having backup SD cards with your project already exported.
