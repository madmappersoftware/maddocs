MadMapper Recent Highlights

Meet MadMapper 6

Geneva, TBA, 2025

MadMapper 6 is now available for purchase on madmapper.com. 

Users who pre-ordered the v6 upgrade can now download it directly from the Downloads section of their Account page.

This major upgrade introduces powerful new tools that let creators explore mapping ideas more freely, perform more intuitively in live settings, and synchronize entire shows with the brand-new Timeline feature. It’s been one of our community’s most requested additions, and we’re thrilled to finally bring it to the MAD universe.

MadMapper’s interface has also received a refined visual overhaul. While everything remains where you expect it, the experience is cleaner and sharper. For a quick tour of what’s new, check out the “MadMapper 6 at a Glance” page.

To support users worldwide, we’re also launching this new MAD Documentation platform, offering a more accessible learning experience than ever. Alongside it, the MAD Certified program is now live on our website, making it easy to find and connect with certified artists, schools, and training partners around the globe.

Welcome to the new era of MadMapper!

 

The MAD team
