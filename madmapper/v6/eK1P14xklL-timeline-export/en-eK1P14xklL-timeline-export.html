Exporting a Timeline from the Conductor

MadMapper allows you to render and ex    port your fully programmed timeline directly from the Conductor. This feature lets you bounce your synchronized video, audio, laser, and DMX sequences into standard files for playback on other systems, archiving, or sharing with clients.

To access these settings, navigate to your Conductor timeline and select the export option.

Export Dialogue Box

At the very top of the Export Timeline dialog, you must choose how much of your timeline you want to render:

  • Export until last key frame / marker: Renders the timeline from the very beginning up to the exact moment of the final programmed event, keyframe, or marker.

  • Export Loop section: Renders only the specific segment of time defined by the active loop brackets on your Conductor timeline.

Export Video Outputs

This section allows you to render your mapped video outputs into standard video files or image sequences.

  • Target Outputs: Select exactly which video outputs you want to render by checking their respective boxes (e.g., Video-Output-1).

  • Include Audio: Check this box next to an output to embed the timeline's audio mix directly into that resulting video file.

  • Quality Slider: Use this to balance file size and visual fidelity when using compressed formats like H.264.

Available Video Codecs:

  • H264 & HEVC: Standard compressed formats, ideal for sharing previews or web delivery.

  • ProRes: High-quality Apple codec, excellent for visually lossless editing and playback on macOS.

  • Hap, HapAlpha, HapQ: GPU-accelerated formats optimized for multi-layer live performance software.

  • Image Sequence: Exports every single frame of your timeline as an individual image file.

Export Laser & DMX Outputs

If your project contains hardware control data, you can export these specialized streams alongside your video.

  • Export Laser Outputs: Bounces your programmed laser paths and generative animations into standard ILDA files for laser playback devices.

  • Export DMX Output: Renders your lighting programming into a new MadLight Recorder Sequence. You also have the option to include a synced audio reference track directly with this data.

Export Audio Output

If you need an isolated audio mixdown of your timeline, enable the audio export section.

  • Audio Codec: Choose between uncompressed WAV for the highest fidelity, or compressed MP3 and M4A formats for smaller file sizes.

  • Audio Channels: Select the number of audio channels (e.g., 2 for a standard stereo mix).

  • Sample Rate: Define the audio resolution (e.g., 44100 Hz for standard CD quality).

Setting the Destination

At the bottom of the window, verify the Export Folder path. This is the directory on your hard drive where all selected video, audio, ILDA, and DMX files will be generated once you click OK. You can use the browse button on the right to designate a specific folder, or click the reset icon to return to the default MadMapper workspace directory.
