Deauthorize MadMapper

Logout From MadMapper

Directly from MadMapper, go to the Account menu at the top of the software/screen > Manage Authorizations:

 

 

Choose whether you want to deauthorize (log out) MadMapper and/or its extensions, then follow the steps in the Authorization Manager as you did when activating the software:

You have the flexibility to deauthorize the software or its extensions on one computer to authorize them on another whenever needed, with no restrictions on the number of times you can switch between devices.

You must also deauthorize MadMapper in the following cases:

- Formatting or erasing a computer
- Activating MadMapper on a new computer if all seats are already in use

Online Reset

If you no longer have access to a computer you need to deauthorize, you can revoke its authorization remotely using a Reset operation.

As the Reset operation is an emergency procedure, its use is limited: if you use a Reset token for one of your licenses, you'll have to wait a year to get your Reset token back for that license.

You can recover a Reset token by launching MadMapper on the computer for which you used the Reset operation.
