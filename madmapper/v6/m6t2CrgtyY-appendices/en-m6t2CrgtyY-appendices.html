Appendices

The following appendices provide detailed technical references, command lists, and hardware specifications for advanced operation.

Appendix A: Complete OSC Command List

A comprehensive table of all Open Sound Control (OSC) addresses available for remote triggering in MadMapper, including timeline controls, opacity faders, and surface geometry adjustments.

Appendix B: Supported DMX & Art-Net Fixtures

A reference guide for patching generic and manufacturer-specific lighting fixtures, including channel breakdown charts for standard RGB, RGBW, and pixel-mapped LED strips.

Appendix C: Laser Hardware & ILDA Specifications

Technical requirements and network setups for supported laser DACs (ShowNET, LaserCube, EtherDream, Helios, FB3/FB4).
