Laser Scanner (MAD Laser Scanner)

Introduced in MadMapper 5.5, the Laser Scanner brings the power of auto-calibration to the MadLaser extension.

Because laser hardware utilizes high-speed mechanical galvanometers (mirrors) rather than pixel grids, aligning laser paths to physical architecture or syncing them perfectly over a video projection traditionally requires intensive, manual Bezier warping.

By utilizing the Laser Scanner, a connected camera reads calibration patterns drawn by your physical laser projector. The software calculates the exact physical distortion, angle, and distance of the laser beam, and automatically generates a corrected mapping. This ensures that your vector paths, ILDA files, and laser materials hit your physical targets with absolute precision.

Using the Laser Scanner

To use the Laser Scanner, you must first have your laser configured properly. If the laser is armed and capable of outputting an image from MadMapper, you can enable the Laser Scanner, which will create a 16x16 matrix of laser dots.

Similar to the Space Scanner, the Laser Scanner will capture images as the rows and columns of dots move. It is crucial that the camera and laser projector do not move during this process. When completed, the Laser Scanner tool will interpret the information and create a custom background image. You will be asked to choose a name and destination for this image, and then it will be automatically displayed in your laser canvas.

 

The image generated will not appear on the final laser output.
