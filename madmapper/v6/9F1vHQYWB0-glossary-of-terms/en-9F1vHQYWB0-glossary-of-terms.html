Glossary of Terms

A quick reference guide for common terminology used in projection mapping, live audiovisual performance, and stage lighting.

 

Art-Net / sACN
Network protocols used to transmit DMX lighting data over standard ethernet cables. Essential for controlling large arrays of modular LED installations without relying on heavy DMX cables.
 
Conductor
MadMapper’s grid-based cueing system, allowing operators to trigger coordinated scenes, lighting changes, and audio tracks simultaneously during a live show.
 
DMX512
The standard digital communication network used to control stage lighting and effects. A single DMX universe contains 512 channels.
 
Generative Light
Lighting or video patterns created in real-time by algorithms or code (such as GLSL shaders) rather than pre-rendered video files, allowing for infinite variations and audio-reactivity.
 
ILDA
The International Laser Display Association. Often refers to the standard analog connection type (DB25 cable) used to transmit vector imagery from a DAC to a laser projector.
 
Projection Mapping
The technique of using specialized software to warp and blend projected video onto complex physical geometry, turning everyday objects or buildings into display surfaces. Also known as Spacial Augmented Reality.
 
Quad
A four-sided polygon surface in MadMapper. The foundational building block for mapping flat rectangular surfaces like screens, windows, or building facades.
