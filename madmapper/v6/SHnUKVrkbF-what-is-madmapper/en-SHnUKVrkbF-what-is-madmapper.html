What Is MadMapper?

 

Created and developed by 1024 architecture and GarageCube since 2010, the concept of MadMapper emerged during an early edition of the Mapping Festival in Geneva, when Frz from 1024 and Boris from GarageCube discussed how to properly map a visual content in real time, in real space.

 

MadMapper was therefore born from the encounter between an artistic collective - 1024 - and a software development company - GarageCube -. The essence of the software comes from this deep relationship between artistic needs and technical solutions to make the dream happen.The core idea is to make the technical aspect of a digital installation as easy as possible, in order to focus on the creative matter rather than spend hours on the setup. 

 

MadMapper is an easy-to-use yet powerful tool to map video into the space, control lights and LED bars, and since version 5, to control and use laser beams in the same intuitive way.

 

Led by 1024 in Paris / France and GarageCube in Geneva / Switzerland, the MAD team has grown to include a developer dream-team based in Brussels / Belgium and an international community of MAD users. The software is used worldwide by countless visual and digital artists, scenographers, stage designers, Vjs, and many others creative people to produce smart digital art installation or big shows in Las Vegas!

 

MadMapper is the tool that we dreamed of.
