Sample Projects & Tutorials

MadMapper is designed to be highly intuitive, but the depth of its features is best explored through hands-on practice. We provide several resources to help you master the software:

 

  • Bundled Templates & Examples: Included with your installation is a curated selection of starting points. By default, when you open MadMapper or create a new project, a dialog box will pop up offering the option to create an empty project or select "From Template". Selecting a template opens pre-configured setups including a Video Wall Template, DMX LED Bar Examples, Laser Examples, and 2-to-4 output SoftEdge Blending configurations. Reverse-engineering these projects is one of the fastest ways to understand MadMapper's routing logic.

Note: This pop-up can be disabled or re-enabled at any time by navigating to MadMapper's Preferences > Application > Startup and toggling "Open Project dialog at startup".

 

  • Learning Resources: For step-by-step guidance, our official YouTube channel hosts a comprehensive library of long-form video tutorials. Additionally, the online Material Library within the software provides access to community-generated shaders and effects.

 

  • Community & Inspiration: Join the official MadMapper Discord and user forums to connect with other creators, troubleshoot workflows, and discuss advanced techniques. You can also follow our Instagram and other social channels for a constant stream of inspiration and creative ideas for your next project.
