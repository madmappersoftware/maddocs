Getting Support

Whether you are troubleshooting a complex routing issue, need help with your license, or just want to connect with other mapping professionals, the MadMapper ecosystem offers several dedicated avenues for support.

In-App Feedback

The fastest and most direct way to get help, report a bug, or share your thoughts with the development team is directly from within the software itself.

  • Send User Feedback: Navigate to the top menu bar in MadMapper and select Help > Send User Feedback. This is highly recommended, as it is the most streamlined way to communicate directly with the MadMapper team while you are actively working in a project.

Direct Support & Contact Form

If you are away from your software, experiencing account/licensing issues, or need to share your .madproject files privately, you can reach out to the development team using the official website.

  • Official Support Page: https://madmapper.com/madmapper/support

  • Best Practices: To get the fastest and most accurate resolution, always include your OS version, MadMapper version, exact hardware specifications (including laser DACs or projectors if applicable), and a clear, step-by-step description of how to reproduce your issue.

Hands-On Assistance & Certified Training

If you need structured learning or hands-on assistance for your specific workflow, connecting with a professional is highly recommended. You can find experienced professionals and officially certified trainers who offer dedicated, one-on-one help to get you up to speed on complex setups, hardware integrations, and advanced show programming.

  • Certified Training Page: https://madmapper.com/learning/certified-training

The Official MadMapper Forum

The MadMapper Forum is the central knowledge base for the community. It is highly active and regularly monitored by the development team and veteran power users.

  • Troubleshooting: Search the archives for existing solutions or post your specific technical questions.

  • Feature Requests: Pitch your ideas for future updates or new tools directly to the developers.

  • Showcase: Share your latest mapping installations, stage designs, and laser shows to inspire others.

  • Forum Link: https://forum.garagecube.com/viewforum.php?f=31

Community Resources & Networks

Beyond the official support tickets, there is a massive global community of projection mapping artists and operators eager to help you learn and troubleshoot.

  • Discord Server & Facebook User Groups: Chat in real-time with other users, ask quick workflow questions, discuss hardware recommendations, and share project assets. You can find the direct invite links to these community networks at the bottom of the main website: https://madmapper.com or for Discord: https://discord.gg/gwhHcBQcFh

  • YouTube Tutorials: The official MadMapper YouTube channel features a deep library of video training, ranging from basic UI navigation to advanced material coding and laser routing. You can access the channel here: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCC9p0fzuYik453n9fXFjpgg
