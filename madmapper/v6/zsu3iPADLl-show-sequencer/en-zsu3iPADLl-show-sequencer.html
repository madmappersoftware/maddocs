Show Sequencer

Store, trigger, animate and sequence everything in your project with Scenes, Cues, Tasks, Grids and Conductor timelines.

The Show Sequencer is where you turn a mapping into a show. It stores the state of surfaces, fixtures, media, modules and outputs, recalls it with transitions, animates any parameter over time, plays video and audio in sync, sends OSC and MIDI, records and plays back DMX, and lets an operator run the whole thing with a single GO button, a controller, a timecode or a schedule.

This section introduces the powerful sequencing and playback tools at the heart of your MadMapper projects. Here you will learn how to sequence your show using timeline-based playback, automate parameters with keyframe interpolation, and perfectly synchronize video and lighting to audio tracks. This section covers everything from managing Scenes and Animation Clips to utilizing the Conductor for a bird's-eye view of your show, and setting up Time Markers for advanced, interactive follow actions.

Changed in 6.2 :
The feature previously called Timelines is now the Show Sequencer. Each bank is now either a Grid (Scenes, Cues and Tasks) or a Conductor (a master timeline), and a Conductor can play Scenes and Cues from any Grid. 6.2 also adds Tasks and the GO button, the Start Point of animated Scenes/Cues, grid orientation flipping, MadLight tracks, OSC Query tracks, equalizer and audio plugins on audio tracks, Link Audio level to surface opacity on Montage clips, and a timecode monitor window for External Sync.
