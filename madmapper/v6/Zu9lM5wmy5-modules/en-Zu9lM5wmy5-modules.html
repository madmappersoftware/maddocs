Modules

Basics

Modules give your access to numerous additional features, settings or controls.

Location

Access the Modules by clicking on Modules (manage Modules) at the top left of the interface:

 

New Module

Simply click on the "+" button to add the Module Instance of your choice:

 

Module Instances List

Added Modules will be listed on the left in the Module Instances List:

 

Activate / De-activate a Module with its blue box.

You can't create Module groups.

Audio Player Module

Allow the use audio files : drag and drop a mp3, wav, aiff, etc. in MadMapper to instantly launch the module and even create cues with it.

Calendar Scheduler

 

Trigger Scenes and Cues at specific dates and times from any calendar application with the Calendar Scheduler Module.

Control Surface

 

Control Surface module lets you visualize, navigate and trigger your Scenes and Cues using one of the currently supported MIDI control surface devices.

AKAI Professional APC mini Mk2

AKAI Professional APC40 Mk2

Novation Launchpad MK2

Launchpad Mini Mk3

Launchpad X

Controls Combiner

 

Mix multiple controls with different combinaison modes.

Cue Scheduler

Schedules your cues/columns with the Cue Scheduler Module.

Device Activity

 

Add numerous controls with the Device Activity module.

Idle Timeline

 

Gives more controls on your Timeline(s).

DMX Router

 

Allow you to route ArtNet / sACN universes to one or multiple USB-DMX devices such as Enttec Pro/mk2 or garageCube USB-DMX.

MadLight Player

 

Play your recorded DMX sequences with ease.

MiniMad Controller

 

While on the same Ethernet network, control your MiniMad units (Video or Light) remotely from with MiniMad controller instances.

MIDI Out

 

With the MIDI Out Module, MadMapper becomes a MIDI controller for your compatible third-party software or devices.

OSC Out

 

The OSC Out Module allows you to send OSC requests from MadMapper.

Oscillator

 

Use this module as a control with numerous signals and parameters.

Oscillator 2D

 

The Oscillator 2D module is identical as the previous one but with two phase settings.

Oscillator Bank

 

Identical as the previous Oscillator module but integrates offset.

Pollution / Weather

    

 

These modules allow you to retrieve data from your environnement such as the temperature, cloudiness, day position, humidity, carbon monoxide, ammonia and more.
